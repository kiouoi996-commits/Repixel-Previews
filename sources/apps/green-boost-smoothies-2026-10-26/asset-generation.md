# ImageGen production prompt set

Built-in ImageGen was used. The design attachment was a visual reference, not an image to flatten into the application. Final saved files are individually linked in the asset manifest and implementation specification. Cup toppings are attached to the physical drink; large surrounding ingredients remain independent alpha assets. WebP conversion changes the color compression only and preserves generated alpha exactly.

- **green-cup.webp** — Product-mockup: isolated clear tapered plastic cup of vivid light-green kiwi-spinach smoothie with tiny black seeds and embedded kiwi slices, open ridged rim, ivory yogurt swirls, small kiwi/walnut/granola topping and mint tuft; pale-green straw leaning 12° right; one white leaf silhouette, no letters; upright front view with slightly visible top, upper-left studio light, 2:3 portrait framing and 5% padding. No external floating fruit or leaves, no floor or UI. Final extraction prompt: keep cup/straw/toppings/logo unchanged; remove all surrounding aura, glow, vignette and backdrop; physical product cutout only, alpha zero outside silhouette.
- **strawberry-cup.webp** — Companion precise-object edit: keep the green cup's silhouette, viewpoint, white leaf logo, lighting and portrait framing; change smoothie to creamy strawberry pink with flecks, pale yogurt, strawberry halves, small mint and a pink straw at the same right-hand position. Final extraction: remove surrounding halo/backdrop; retain cup and contents unchanged; tight transparent cutout for white cards.
- **mango-cup.webp** — Product-mockup: isolated clear tapered takeaway cup of golden-orange mango smoothie, thick mango yogurt, neat mango cubes and one mint sprig, open ridged rim, white leaf silhouette, pale-gold straw at right angled 12°; upright front view, slightly visible top, 2:3 framing, soft upper-left studio light. No external fruit, floor, backdrop, glow, typography or UI; transparent alpha.
- **berry-cup.webp** — Product-mockup: isolated clear tapered cup of purple blueberry smoothie, lavender yogurt top, five dusty indigo blueberries, small mint tuft, lavender straw at right, one white leaf silhouette low on front. Same frontal slight-top perspective and 2:3 framing, 5% padding, upper-left light. No floating ingredients, backdrop or UI; genuine transparency.
- **kiwi.webp** — One round kiwi cross-section, juicy lime flesh, radial black seeds, ivory core, thin fuzzy brown peel; nearly frontal with 8° tilt and a little peel visible at lower-left; whole slice, 12% square-canvas padding, natural studio food light, alpha, no shadow or other fruit.
- **banana.webp** — One thick peeled banana coin, pale cream/yellow flesh, subtle five-point seed center; slightly oblique cut face and visible thickness, −20° face rotation, 15% square-canvas padding, upper-left light, alpha, no floor/shadow/other fruit.

The generated product imagery reconstructs the visible material/lighting language; hidden photographic pixels and exact source-object identities are not recoverable from the screenshot.
