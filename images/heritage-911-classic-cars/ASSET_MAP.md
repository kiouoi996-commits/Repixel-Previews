## Layer separation

The photos retain their own studio backgrounds and contact shadows. Render all UI labels, pills, circles, arrows, likes, pagination, cards and panels as separate components. Do not duplicate photographic shadows with CSS. SVGs have transparent backgrounds, and button circles are implemented in CSS.
