## Rendering notes

- Cart quantities, favorite hearts, navigation controls, button circles, food descriptions, labels, badge `30% OFF`, and prices remain HTML/CSS.
- The hero bowl is partially occluded by the badge in the source; the unseen arc is alpha-transparent. Position the badge over this area.
- CSS category circular pads are separate from the food cutouts.
- Soft contact shadows and pale photo backgrounds are retained inside image content; do not duplicate as CSS shadows.
- Header/page gradients and cards are specified in `manifest.json`.
- Some food/photo subjects cannot be perfectly recovered from low-resolution screenshot pixels.
